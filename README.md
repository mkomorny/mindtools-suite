A comprehensive AI-powered cognitive assistance and communication studio featuring 15+ specialized mental model tools. Designed to help engineers, creators, and leaders sharpen thinking, audit biases, optimize prompts, and communicate effectively.

## Built-in Cognitive Tools

- **Steelman Machine**: Transforms opposing viewpoints into their strongest, most compelling form.
- **Cognitive Bias Auditor**: Inspects arguments, decisions, and strategies for common cognitive fallacies and blind spots.
- **ELI5 Machine**: Deconstructs dense technical or scientific concepts into intuitive analogies.
- **Tone & Register Translator**: Seamlessly rewrites text across diplomatic, executive, casual, or assertive registers.
- **Prompt Forger & Optimizer**: Generates, refines, and stress-tests LLM system prompts with token and cost tracking.
- **Comm Assistant & Vent Session**: Drafts constructive communications from unstructured thoughts.
- **Subtext Readout & Conversation Simulator**: Previews interpersonal dynamics and anticipates conversational reactions.
